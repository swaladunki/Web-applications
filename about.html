Our Journey

From a humble street cart to becoming one of Bangalore's most beloved egg rice destinations.

Uncle Egg Rice Corner began with a simple vision: to serve delicious, affordable egg-based dishes to the hardworking people of Bangalore. What started as a small street-side cart near PES Degree College in Banashankari has grown into a legendary spot that draws food lovers from across the city.

Our founder, affectionately known as "Uncle," brought authentic street food flavors and generous portions to the neighborhood. Students, office workers, and families quickly discovered that our egg rice wasn't just food – it was an experience of genuine Bangalore street food culture.

The Uncle Egg Rice Legacy

Over the years, we've stayed true to our roots while continuously improving our recipes and service. Each dish is prepared with the same care and attention that made us famous. We believe in:

  • Fresh Ingredients: We source fresh eggs and vegetables daily to ensure the highest quality.
  • Authentic Flavors: Our spice blends and cooking techniques preserve the authentic taste of traditional street food.
  • Generous Portions: We believe in giving our customers great value – less price, high quantity, and uncompromising taste.
  • Quick Service: Our efficient preparation ensures you get hot, fresh food without long waits.
  • Affordable Pricing: Quality food shouldn't be expensive. We keep our prices accessible to everyone.
