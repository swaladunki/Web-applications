Our Story

ನಮ್ಮ ಕಥೆ

Welcome to Uncle Egg Rice Corner, Bangalore's beloved street food destination that has been serving delicious, spicy egg rice and authentic egg dishes for years. Located in the heart of Banashankari, near PES Degree College, we've become a legendary spot among students, office-goers, and street food lovers.

What started as a humble street cart has grown into one of the most reviewed and loved street food spots in Bengaluru. Our secret? Simple, fresh ingredients, generous portions, and authentic street food flavors that remind you of home-cooked meals.

Whether you're craving our signature spicy egg rice, crispy egg bonda, or flavorful egg chilli, we serve it hot, fresh, and with the warmth of traditional Bangalore street food culture.
